Daily use

  1. Open Plan routes. Pick the delivery date. Clients who usually order that day are already checked.
  2. Check or uncheck stops to match today's orders. Tick Priority for anything that must arrive early and set its Deliver by time.
  3. Choose the number of drivers. Check each driver's hours so far this week (filled in from History).
  4. Click Build routes. Compare driver counts shows one driver fewer and one more, with labor (overtime at 1.5×) and fuel. The cheapest on-time plan is marked Best. Click a row to see it.
  5. Need a change? Use ▲ ▼ to reorder a stop, or Move to to give it to another driver. Times and cost update right away.
  6. Drivers see their route as soon as they log in on their phone (or send a phone link / print the sheets). Late order? Use Add a late order — drivers get the update within a couple of minutes.

Accounts

  • Admin: everything, including Settings and Users (add people, reset passwords).
  • Secretary: clients, today's stops, building and sending routes, History.
  • Driver: logs in on a phone and sees only their own route. Their Done ticks show on the office screen.
  • Use Change password at the top to set your own password. Log out on shared computers and phones.

How cost is figured

  • Drivers are paid from clock-in (8:00 AM) until they are back at the warehouse. Every extra driver adds their morning hours, so sometimes a little overtime is cheaper than another driver. The planner works that out.
  • Overtime (1.5×): time past 8 hours in a day, or past 40 hours for the week (Texas follows the federal 40-hour rule). Hours already worked this week come from History.
  • Fuel: miles × fuel cost per mile (Settings).
  • Nobody is planned to come back after the Latest return time.

Good to know

  • Drive times come from Google with typical midday traffic and are saved, so planning a normal day costs nothing. Only new clients trigger a small lookup.
  • Lines on the map are straight lines between stops, not the actual roads. Google Maps gives the turn-by-turn route.
  • Every plan you build is saved in History under its date (the last one built or changed counts).

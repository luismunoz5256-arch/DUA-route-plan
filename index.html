Daily use

  1. Open Plan routes. Pick the delivery date. Clients who usually order that day are already checked.
  2. Check or uncheck stops to match today's orders. Tick Priority for anything that must arrive early and set its Deliver by time.
  3. Choose the number of drivers and click Build routes.
  4. Look at Compare driver counts: the cheapest plan that gets everyone back on time is marked Best. Click a row to see it.
  5. Print the route sheets, or use Open in Google Maps / Copy for text on each driver card to send it to the driver's phone.

Good to know

  • Drive times come from Google with typical midday traffic and are saved, so planning a normal day costs nothing. Only new clients trigger a small lookup.
  • If there is no Google key (or the monthly limit is reached), the planner uses straight-line estimates and says so.
  • Lines on the map are straight lines between stops, not the actual roads. Google Maps gives the turn-by-turn route.
  • Google Maps links hold up to 9 stops each, so longer routes get "Part 1", "Part 2" links.
  • Client "earliest / latest" hours are optional. The driver will wait if they arrive before a client opens.
